The first public build of **Dreadhold**, a dark-fantasy, Doom-style first-person survival game:
swords, sorcery and bows, no guns. Every texture, sprite, sound and music track is generated by code.

**▶ No download needed: [play in your browser](https://dreadhold.vercel.app)**

## Download

| Platform | File | How to start |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10/11, 64-bit | `Dreadhold-v0.1.0-windows-x86_64.zip` | Unzip, run `Dreadhold.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit | `Dreadhold-v0.1.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | Extract, run `./Dreadhold.x86_64` |

- The builds are not code-signed. On Windows, SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"*:
  click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- A graphics card with Vulkan or Direct3D 12 support is recommended.

## What's in it

- **Three champions**: Warrior (Rage: Cleave, Reaving Spin, Iron Charge), Mage (Mana: Fireball, Frost Nova,
  Blink) and Hunter (Focus: Arrow Shot, Volley, Shadow Step), each with three weapon tiers won from bosses.
- **The Vigil**: survive night after night on four battlefields (Blackstone Keep, the Sunken Crypt,
  Hollowmere, the Defiled Cathedral).
- **Four bosses** in their own arenas, every fifth night: the Hollow King, the Mother of Rot,
  the Cinder Tyrant and Lady Rime, each with three phases.
- **Grizzle's black market** at dawn: abilities, potions, healing and a torch for warriors.
- **Duo vigils**: online 2-player co-op through a room code, between the app and the browser alike.
  Fallen champions can be raised by their ally.
- **Saves** at every dawn in solo, with Continue from the title screen.
- **A full soundtrack**: night music that grows heavier every five nights, a theme per boss, the goblin's
  dawn tune and an ambience for every battlefield.

## Controls

`WASD` (or `ZQSD` on AZERTY) to move, mouse to aim, `Left` / `Right click` for your abilities, `E` / `Shift`
for the utility ability, `Space` to jump, `R` for a potion, `F` to talk to the goblin or raise an ally,
`Esc` for the menu.
