**Dreadhold v0.2.0: the endless vigil.** The nights no longer run out of surprises: weapons, monsters,
battlefields and bosses are now forged without end, the dead grow far stronger, and you can hold the line
with an ally online.

**▶ [Play in your browser](https://dreadhold.vercel.app)**, or download the desktop build below.

## Download

| Platform | File | How to start |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10/11, 64-bit | `Dreadhold-v0.2.0-windows-x86_64.zip` | Unzip, run `Dreadhold.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit | `Dreadhold-v0.2.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | Extract, run `./Dreadhold.x86_64` |

The builds are not code-signed: on Windows, if SmartScreen says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New in this version

- **Duo vigils (online co-op)**: open a room in *the Duo Hall*, give its four-letter code to an ally, and play
  together, browser and desktop app alike. Fallen champions are down, not dead: hold `F` next to them to raise them.
- **The weapon forge**: every boss offers three generated weapons: 11 bases × 9 materials (and beyond) ×
  6 enchantments (burning, frost, venom, chain lightning, shadow, holy) × properties, coloured in hand by their
  material and enchantment.
- **The monster forge**: from night 3, every night brings its own species built from modular parts (body, head,
  armour, weapon, off-hand, elemental aura) with traits: swift, ironclad, bloodthirsty, volatile, splitting,
  undying, phasing, frenzied, hulking.
- **The arena forge**: *Uncharted Lands* builds a new battlefield for every vigil, from six themes.
- **The boss forge**: after the four legends (nights 5 to 20), every boss is generated, with its own mechanics,
  name, arena and music.
- **Grizzle never runs dry**: generated charms fill his stock; his prices rise every night.
- **A much harder vigil**: the dead grow stronger, faster and more numerous every night, and golden elites
  appear from night 6.
- **Champions seen by your ally**: full sprites for the warrior, the mage and the hunter.
- **A new soundtrack**: night music that grows heavier every five nights, a theme per boss, dawn and defeat
  tunes, an ambience per battlefield, and every sound effect redone.

## Fixes

- Sound now plays in the browser, and the music loops there.
- No foe survives the end of a night anymore (minions rising from the ground used to outlive their boss).
- Map cards keep their text inside their frame.
